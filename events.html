Community Events

Celebrations that unite us.

From the first rays of the sun on Suryanamaskar Day to the joyful celebrations throughout the year, every event at our centre strengthens the bonds of our community and brings us closer together.

सूर्यनमस्कार दिन
Suryanamaskar Day

A vibrant annual celebration dedicated to Suryanamaskar — the complete salutation to the sun. Members of all batches gather at sunrise to perform 108 rounds in unison, honouring the sun's life-giving energy and the strength it builds within each of us. A high-energy, joyful morning that fills everyone with positivity for the day ahead.

योगदिन
International Yoga Day · 21 June

Every year on 21st June, we celebrate International Yoga Day with great enthusiasm — our largest community event. Members come together to embrace the benefits of yoga and experience its transformative power through a joyful and rejuvenating celebration.

सहल · स्नेहमेळावा
Annual Outing & Get-Together

Every year, each batch goes on a happiness trip — our beloved Sahal and Snehamelava. This annual outing is a time to step away from routine, breathe in nature, deepen friendships, and celebrate the bonds formed through shared practice on the mat. It is not just a trip — it is a reunion of hearts. A cherished tradition that members look forward to all year long.
